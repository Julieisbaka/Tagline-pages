Your research belongs to you

A vault is a folder.
Not a lock-in.

Tagline works with ordinary folders and Markdown files. Notes, speech flows, and canvases live in your vault, so you can keep and back up your work with the tools you already use.

Optional integrations and local models are there when you need them. Your core workspace stays yours.
